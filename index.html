Every PDF job,
one place.

Merge, split, reorder, number, watermark and convert PDFs in seconds. No account, no waiting on a server.

  1. Pick a toolSearch below, or choose one from the tool list.
  2. Add your PDFDrop it in the box. It stays open in every tool, so you can do several things in a row.
  3. SaveYour new file downloads. Click "Keep editing it" to carry on with another tool.

      Organize pages

      Drag to reorder. Click pages to select them, then rotate, copy or remove them together.

      No pages selected Double-click a page to zoom in

      Split / extract

      Break a PDF into several files, or pull out only the pages you need.

      Separate with commas. 8- means page 8 to the end.

      Several files are saved together in one zip.

      Crop & resize

      Trim the edges of your pages, or change them to another paper size.

      Changing the paper size keeps how the page looks, but links on the page stop working.

      Sign

      Draw, type or upload your signature, then drag it where it belongs.

      Drag to move. Drag the corner to resize. Press Delete to remove the selected item.

      Fill forms

      Fill in the boxes of a fillable PDF form, then save it.

      Compress

      Make a PDF smaller so it is easier to email and upload.

      Recommended and Strong turn each page into a picture, so text can no longer be selected or searched.

      Page numbers

      Stamp a number on every page.

      Batch

      Do the same thing to many PDFs at once, and get them all back in one zip.

        Removes document details, hidden XMP data, scripts, attached files, comments and app data from every file.

        Password protect

        Lock a PDF so it only opens with the password you choose.

        The file is encrypted with AES-256. Keep the password somewhere safe: nobody, including this site, can recover it for you.

        Remove password

        Make an unlocked copy of a PDF you have the password for.

        Remove hidden data

        Find and remove the details a PDF carries without showing them, before you share it.

        Bookmarks

        The clickable table of contents that PDF readers show in their side panel.

          Use the arrows to put a bookmark inside the one above it (a chapter's sections, for example).

          Compare

          Two versions of a document, side by side: see what was added, removed or moved.

          Changed

          Extract text

          All the text of a PDF, ready to copy or save as a text file.

          Extract images

          Every picture inside the PDF, at the size it is stored.

          Scan to text (OCR)

          Reads the words in scanned pages so you can search, select and copy them.

          Languages in the document

          The reading engine (about 5 MB, plus each language) is downloaded the first time, so this tool needs an internet connection. Your PDF itself is never uploaded. Searchable PDFs work for Latin-script languages; Arabic text is given as a text file.

          Annotate

          Add text, highlights, boxes, arrows and drawings to your pages.

          Header & footer

          Put text along the top and bottom of every page: a title, a date, page numbers.

          Header (top of the page) Footer (bottom of the page)

          You can use {page}, {pages}, {date} and {file}. They are filled in on each page.

          Redact

          Black out private details for good: the hidden text is removed, not just covered.

          Pages with black boxes are saved as pictures, so nothing under the boxes can be copied or recovered. Other pages stay as they are.

          Watermark

          Put text such as CONFIDENTIAL, or your logo, across your pages.

          Document info

          See and change the title, author and other details stored inside the PDF.

          PDF to images

          Save pages as pictures. Several pages are saved together in one zip.
