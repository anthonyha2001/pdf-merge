      Organize pages

      Drag to reorder. Click pages to select them, then rotate, copy or remove them together.

      Split / extract

      Break a PDF into several files, or pull out only the pages you need.

      Separate with commas. 8- means page 8 to the end.

      Several files are saved together in one zip.

      Crop & resize

      Trim the edges of your pages, or change them to another paper size.

      Changing the paper size keeps how the page looks, but links on the page stop working.

      Sign

      Draw, type or upload your signature, then drag it where it belongs.

      Drag to move. Drag the corner to resize. Press Delete to remove the selected item.

      Fill forms

      Fill in the boxes of a fillable PDF form, then save it.

        Extract images

        Scan to text (OCR)

        Reads the words in scanned pages so you can search, select and copy them.

        Languages in the document

        The reading engine (about 5 MB, plus each language) is downloaded the first time, so this tool needs an internet connection. Your PDF itself is never uploaded. Searchable PDFs work for Latin-script languages; Arabic text is given as a text file.

        Annotate

        Add text, highlights, boxes, arrows and drawings to your pages.

        Header & footer

        Put text along the top and bottom of every page: a title, a date, page numbers.

        Header (top of the page) Footer (bottom of the page)

        You can use {page}, {pages}, {date} and {file}. They are filled in on each page.

        Redact

        Black out private details for good: the hidden text is removed, not just covered.

        Pages with black boxes are saved as pictures, so nothing under the boxes can be copied or recovered. Other pages stay as they are.

        Watermark

        Put text such as CONFIDENTIAL, or your logo, across your pages.

        Document info

        See and change the title, author and other details stored inside the PDF.

        PDF to images

        Save pages as pictures. Several pages are saved together in one zip.
